SMA Crossover

Tracks the 50-day moving average against the 200-day. When the shorter average crosses above the longer one — a Golden Cross — it signals an uptrend and buys in. When it crosses back below — a Death Cross — it exits. Strong in sustained trends, weak in sideways markets.

RSI

Measures momentum on a 0–100 scale. Buys when RSI drops below 30 (oversold), sells above 70 (overbought). Looked like a standout performer on AAPL alone — but the edge collapsed once tested across 9 tickers. A classic overfitting trap.

Buy & Hold

The simplest possible strategy: buy on day one, hold through every rally and every drawdown, never touch it again. No timing, no signals, no trading fees. This is the baseline every "smarter" strategy above has to actually beat — and often doesn't.
